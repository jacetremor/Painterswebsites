# SEO Content, Location, Service, Image, and Redirect Guides

## SEO content entry

Write the page purpose, search intent, title, description, H1, navigation label, and opening copy independently. The title should identify the useful subject and market; the H1 should orient the visitor; the navigation label should remain concise. Avoid keyword repetition that makes sentences unnatural. Link to the next useful service, location, project, or contact step using descriptive anchor text.

AI-assisted content stays in draft. Supply real customer questions, painter advice, products, techniques, completed projects, observations, and original photos. Review every factual claim. Never invent neighborhoods served, credentials, testimonials, projects, prices, ratings, team biographies, or product experience.

## Location-page quality

A publishable location page needs an actual local reason to exist: a completed project, original local photograph, verified testimonial, real neighborhood or housing observation, climate/surface note, scheduling constraint, service limitation, or first-party customer question. Confirm city/state, available services, canonical, metadata, and nearby links. A city-name swap is not original local content. Keep the page draft or noindex when the evidence is weak.

## Service-page quality

Explain where the service fits, how surface condition changes preparation, what products or systems may be considered, the work sequence, common concerns, and the limits of remote advice. Link relevant projects and locations. Do not publish a thin variant solely to capture a keyword. Product names and claims require painter verification.

## Image SEO

Use an original, permission-cleared image near the text it supports. Rename it descriptively before upload, preserve width/height, compress to AVIF/WebP where practical, and avoid oversized hero sources. Describe visible, relevant information in alt text; use an explicit decorative decision and empty alt for decorative images. Captions can add project, surface, location, stage, and product context. Before/after pairs need accurate labels and matching viewpoints where possible.

## Redirect management

When a published slug changes, create one 301/308 from the old path to the final canonical path. Test for loops and chains before activation. Never redirect an unrelated removed page to the homepage; return 410 when the content is intentionally gone without a replacement. Update internal links and sitemap output to the final URL.
